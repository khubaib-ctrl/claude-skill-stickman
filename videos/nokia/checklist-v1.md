## Uncertain items

1. **Word timings are estimates.** VoiceStudio returns no word timestamps, so
   caption highlights and beat timing are spread by syllable within each speech
   region. They can drift by a fraction of a second within a line.
2. **Pronunciation hasn't been heard by a person.** Two lines need a listen:
   - **Line 5** ("bet its smartphones"): the isolated line transcribed
     correctly, but the full-video pass heard "bared".
   - **Line 6** ("phones sold"): transcribed as "phone sold".
3. **The Gartner Q3 2011 figure (23.9%) is from a single analyst**, read via a
   Reuters wire report. That's a reliable chain, but it's one firm.
4. **2012 unit sales differ by 1 million across reports** (333M vs 334M). The
   narration says "almost ninety million fewer", which is true for both.
5. **Nokia's present-day status** ("builds mobile networks") is backed by its
   2016 Alcatel-Lucent acquisition. Its current business wasn't re-checked for
   2026.
6. **The TechCrunch source** (2007 sales context) was seen only in search
   results. No narration line depends on it.

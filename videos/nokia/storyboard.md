## What changed in v2 (feedback: more engaging script)

- **A cold open that sets the question.** The video starts with the end: "Less
  than six years later, that company sold off its phone business. This is how
  Nokia lost."
- **Real quotes from the memo**, read in the narration and shown on screen, in
  place of the narrator paraphrasing them.
- **The memo's burning-oil-platform story staged as a symbolic scene,** a big
  visual set piece.
- **A running joke built from facts:** the CEO came from Microsoft, then went
  back to Microsoft with the sale.
- **Shorter, punchier sentences with rhetorical turns:** "What could go wrong?",
  "The buyer? Microsoft."
- **Sound design:** an original background score and synced sound effects
  (marked 🔊 below).
- Every new fact is in `sources.md` (sources 11–12, rows marked v2).

  setup → punchline action and are marked as cuts.
- At most one camera move per scene.

  "From Microsoft", €5.44B, $7.6B, and the final beat.

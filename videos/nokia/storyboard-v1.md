# How Nokia Lost the Phone Market in Under 6 Years: storyboard

## Title

**"How Nokia Lost the Phone Market in Under 6 Years"** (option B, approved).
The original working title, "How Nokia Went From #1 to Nothing in 5 Years",
was dropped because Nokia still exists and peak-to-sale took about 5 years
9 months.

## Recommended duration: about 80 seconds

This is longer than a 60-second Short, and on purpose:
- The story needs **five dated beats** to make sense: the 2007 peak, the iPhone,
  the 2011 memo and Windows Phone bet, the 2011–12 slide, and the 2013 sale.
  It also has a **two-part ending** (Microsoft's 2015 write-off, and Nokia
  surviving as a networks company).
- **Four numbers need a short hold** to land: 40%, the €5.44B deal, 32,000
  employees and the $7.6B charge.
- Cutting to 60 s would mean dropping either the memo (the turning point) or the
  write-off (the payoff).
- 176 narration words at 2.3 wps is about 77 s, plus about 5 s of holds, which
  gives about 80 s (82 s at most). The cloned voice runs faster (~2.9 wps), so the measured
  audio will probably come in around 60–65 s. Shots get resized to the real
  audio in production.

## Pacing check

- Every scene changes a pose, prop or framing every 2–4 s.
  - Scenes 6 and 10 use stepped "snaps" to keep long lines moving.
- One main action per scene.
- Six scenes have no camera move. The rest have one push-in or pan.
- Holds on the four key numbers (scenes 1, 8 and 10) and on the final beat
  (scene 11).
